## 1. Radiologist

```
RADIOLOGIST VERDICT: APPROVE WITH CHANGES
```

**Orientation and laterality.** No blocker found. On the sample the axial view is in radiological convention (R on screen left), A/P/R/L/S/I markers are on all three slice views, "Left lung" lies on the patient's left in axial, coronal and 3D, and its volume (3,083 mL) is smaller than the right (3,613 mL), as expected. Nothing in 0.1.2 touches geometry: maximise changes zoom only, "Smooth pixels" switches display interpolation only (`src/viewer/engine.ts:723`), window and zoom are display only. A view-through-host guest gets the host's grid, spacing and direction and shows the same markers. Oblique, anisotropic, DICOM and non-NIfTI input: not verified in this review (unit tests exist).

**CT presets** (`src/viewer/window.ts:31`, `DECISIONS.md` 2026-11-21). The citation to Radiopaedia "Windowing (CT)" matches the values as I know them; I could not open the page from here, so the citation text itself is not verified.

| Preset | W / L | View |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soft tissue | 400 / 40 | Standard. |
| Abdomen | 350 / 50 | Standard, inside the usual 350–400 / 40–60. Nearly the same picture as Soft tissue (−125…225 HU against −160…240 HU). Chest readers will look for "Mediastinum", which is this window. |
| Liver | 150 / 30 | Standard as published, and right for unenhanced liver. On a portal-venous scan the liver (about 100–130 HU) sits at or above the white end (105 HU), so enhanced parenchyma is clipped. Acceptable; questionable as the only liver window. |
| Pancreas | 150 / 65 | **Questionable.** Not a standard named preset: it is not in the Radiopaedia list, and the one source is a paper on a pancreatitis severity index, not a windowing reference. That paper gives W 150 with L 50–80; L 65 is a midpoint chosen here, not a published value. The window shows −10…140 HU, which is a reasonable narrow window for a contrast-enhanced pancreas and does no harm, but on an unenhanced scan (gland about 30–50 HU) the pancreas is dark grey. A typical abdominal radiologist reads the pancreas on the abdomen window (W 350–400, L 40–50) and narrows by hand, to roughly W 150–250 with L 60–100 on a pancreatic-phase scan, when looking for a low-attenuation lesion. They would not expect a preset called Pancreas, and would not agree on its numbers. |
| Lung | 1500 / −600 | Standard. |
| Bone | 1800 / 400 | Standard. |
| Brain | 80 / 40 | Standard. |
| Stroke | 40 / 40 | Standard (8 / 32 is the common alternative). |
| Subdural | 200 / 75 | Standard, inside 130–300 / 50–100. |

The ranges I give for hand-narrowed pancreas and enhanced liver windows are reading practice, not a cited source.

**MR presets** (`mrWindows`, `src/viewer/window.ts:177`). Working them out from percentiles is the right approach, because MR values have no unit. The names are the problem. "Soft tissue" is also a CT preset with a different meaning. "Bright fluid" describes T2; on T1 the brightest tissue is fat, and what the preset does is simply "clip nothing bright". Not verified on a real MR volume.

**Can a new tool silently damage a mask?**

- Scissors, all slices: acts on the active segment only, refuses locked and hidden segments, and answers with a count and Undo. Good.
- Erase outside, this slice: no message (`src/session.ts:1124` toasts only for all slices). When zoomed in, it removes parts of the segment on this slice that are off screen, unseen.
- Remove island: on a segment that is one island, one click removes the whole segment (3,083 mL of lung in the test). A toast with Undo follows. Acceptable, but it reads as "remove a stray piece".
- Keep island, split: counted, undoable. Keep island on the sample correctly removed a 6 mm³ speck.
- Fill between slices: previewed as stripes, written only on Apply, empty voxels only. Good.
- Toast Undo buttons call plain undo (`src/session.ts:996`–`1320`), so the button on an older toast undoes whatever was done last.

**View-through-host guest against the host.** Not safe to read from, and it does not say so clearly enough:

- The guest's value read-out shows the 8-bit grey level with no unit ("Value 84" where the host shows "−906 HU").
- The guest's window read-out says "W 255 L 128 Full range". The guest cannot tell which window the host has chosen, and so cannot tell lung from bone window except by eye.
- The guest's window menu is broken (finding M2): Auto and the three MR presets are all "W 1 L 0.5", and the CT list offers Hounsfield windows for 0–255 data.
- The threshold brush limits are 0 and 1 on 0–255 data (M2).
- Volumes and measurements are correctly withheld from guests.
- The 3D view shows only the three fetched planes, as a cross. It looks like a damaged mask.
- Slab darkening against the host (`DECISIONS.md`, neighbours fetched): by eye the guest and host pictures matched; not measured.

**Rooms.** Presence avatars are shown. I did not see another person's cursor on the image in any two-person screenshot; remote cursors (`DESIGN.md` 4.8): not verified. Merge after offline work on different slices worked. The conflict review (*Use mine* / *Keep the room's*) was not driven by hand; its e2e tests pass.

**Wording.** "W Annotator is not for diagnosis or treatment" and the de-identify line are in the room dialog. Good.

Cases I would test with: an unenhanced and a pancreatic-phase abdomen CT (presets), a T1 and a T2 brain MR (preset names), a CT cropped to soft tissue with no air (CT detection `min ≤ −500 and max > 300`), a left/right asymmetric phantom through a view-through-host room.
