# Decisions

A running record of decisions made about W Annotator: by the user, in a `grill-me` session, by the council, or by an agent that had to choose in order to continue.

How to use it:

- Add one row per decision, newest at the bottom. Use the date the decision was made (YYYY-MM-DD).
- Write the decision as a plain statement someone can act on. Give the reason in one line.
- Never edit or delete an earlier row. To change a decision, add a new row that says which one it supersedes.
- Check here before asking a question. If it is answered, do not ask again.

| 2026-11-21 | Rooms v2: how does someone get the image and mask on joining a shared-copy room? | They ask the host, or the next person online after 5 s, who sends 16-slice slabs compressed one at a time in parts of at most 1 MiB, paced to half the relay's allowance. | 2026-11-21 | Rooms v2: how is offline work merged? | Each member of a shared-copy room keeps a second copy of the mask as the room has confirmed it. There is no per-slice review, no outline of my version, and no 7-day copy of the pre-merge mask. | The confirmed copy is exactly the merge base, so nothing has to be reconstructed; it costs one mask of memory. | 2026-11-21 | Which CT windows are built in, and where do the numbers come from? | Soft tissue 400/40, Abdomen 350/50, Liver 150/30, Pancreas 150/65, Lung 1500/−600, Bone 1800/400, Brain 80/40, Stroke 40/40, Subdural 200/75 (width/level, HU), abdominal ones together. | 2026-11-21 | What are the MR presets? | Windows worked out from the open image, named for what they do: Auto (0.5th–99.5th percentile of all values, as before), Full range (smallest to largest value), Soft tissue (2nd–90th percentile of the tissue), Bright fluid (2nd percentile of the tissue to the largest value), Dark detail (2nd–50th percentile of the tissue). Up to 200,003 evenly spaced voxels are looked at. | MR values have no unit, so fixed numbers are wrong, and the sequence cannot be told from the file. 90% clips the brightest tenth (fluid on T2, fat on T1); the largest value keeps bright fluid unclipped; the median bounds the darker half. | 2026-11-21 | Which preset list does an image get? | The CT list when the values look like Hounsfield units (`image.ct`: minimum ≤ −500 and maximum > 300), otherwise the MR list. The preset keys step through the list on show. | The guess can be wrong (a CT cropped to soft tissue, an MR with negative values), and the user must not be stuck with it. |
| 2026-11-21 | How are saved window presets kept? | In settings (`windowPresets`), as name, width and level; at most 12; names up to 24 characters; a name already used by a built-in or saved preset is refused with the reason.
