# Code of conduct

This project is a place to work on software. Everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to treat the others with respect.

## What we expect

Be courteous and assume good intent. Criticise the work, not the person. Accept that people have different backgrounds and levels of experience, and that a question you find obvious may not be obvious to someone else. Keep discussion on the topic of the project.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, personal attacks, sexual or discriminatory language or imagery, threats, and publishing someone's private information without permission. Repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop is also not acceptable. Never post patient data or anything that could identify a patient.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks these rules, tell the maintainer, [Ario Moniri](https://github.com/ArioMoniri), through GitHub. You can use the "Report content" option on the comment or issue, or contact the maintainer through their profile if you prefer not to post publicly. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept private.

## What happens next

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and warn or block people who break these rules. Decisions are made case by case, with the aim of keeping the project a place where people can work.
